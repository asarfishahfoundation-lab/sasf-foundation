ABOUT SASF

People, purpose & progress.

Shree Asarfi Shah Foundation is a people-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities, supporting communities, and contributing toward a better and more equitable future.

Our Story

Building Hope Through Action

Shree Asarfi Shah Foundation was established with a commitment to creating meaningful opportunities and supporting communities through education, social development and compassionate action.

Our work is rooted in the belief that lasting change begins when people come together, support one another and create opportunities for a better future.

Through community-focused initiatives and responsible partnerships, SASF aims to make a positive and lasting contribution to society.

OUR FOUNDATION Built around people and opportunity.
OUR FOUNDER

Er. Raghbendra Kumar Shah

Electrical engineer, investor and social worker.

His involvement in social service dates back to 2010. His educational journey includes studies in both Janakpur, Nepal and Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Through the foundation, his vision is to contribute toward communities by supporting education, social development, vulnerable individuals, and initiatives that can create meaningful opportunities for future generations.
